# Research licensing and provenance boundary

The `Research/` directory contains preserved research artifacts, including
LCRD-authored notes, extracted factual observations, source URLs, citations,
historical passes, and intermediate audit material.

This directory is intentionally not blanket-licensed. Original LCRD-authored
research notes, factual observations, source records, URLs, citations, hashes,
and research data may be covered by CC BY 4.0 where LIMNMEDIA LLC or
contributors have the rights to license them. Third-party works are not
relicensed merely because they are referenced, summarized, or preserved here.

In particular, manufacturer specifications, linked documentation, copied text,
third-party datasets, photographs, sample images, RAW/JPEG files, logos,
trademarks, product names, and other externally authored works retain their
original rights and status.
Source and provenance records identify where a fact came from; they do not
claim ownership of the underlying source material.

The canonical LCRD data under `data/` and generated distributions under
`dist/` are licensed under CC BY 4.0 only to the extent LIMNMEDIA LLC and
contributors have rights to license those contents. Consumers must preserve
the provenance and any applicable third-party rights distinctions.
