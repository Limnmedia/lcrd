# 0001 — Observations versus interpretations

## Context

Sources and research passes contain facts, measurements, labels, and uncertain
claims. Consumers may need different interpretations of the same evidence.

## Decision

LCRD preserves source-backed observations and derives normalized reference
entities and convenience distributions from them. It does not replace an
observation with a consumer-specific calculation.

## Why

Keeping the raw value, method, confidence, and source makes corrections and
independent review possible. Consumers remain free to calculate crop factor,
field of view, pixel pitch, or application-specific matches.

## Consequences

The data contains more provenance and occasional unresolved records, but it
does not claim certainty that the source did not provide.
