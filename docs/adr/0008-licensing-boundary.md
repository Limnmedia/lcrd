# 0008 — Licensing boundary

## Context

The repository contains original data, software infrastructure, documentation,
and preserved research that may reference third-party works.

## Decision

Original LCRD data, distributions, and documentation use CC BY 4.0. Original
tools, tests, schemas, workflows, and build infrastructure use Apache-2.0.
Third-party source material in `Research/` retains its original rights/status
and is not blanket-relicensed.

## Why

The boundary lets downstream users identify the applicable license without
pretending LCRD owns manufacturer documents, media, trademarks, or copied
third-party works.

## Consequences

Contributors must preserve provenance and only contribute material they have
the right to redistribute. See the root `LICENSE` and `Research/LICENSING.md`.
